About me.

Mobile developer, UiTM Mobile Computing graduate, two-time gold award winner. The longer story, skills and all.

Hi, I'm Nizam — a Mobile Developer at Zen Computer System Sdn Bhd. I build production iOS and Android apps with a focus on cross-platform development with Flutter and native platform optimisation.

How I work

01

I own the release, not just the feature

Both of my production apps went to the App Store and Google Play with me running the release. I set up the iOS build from scratch for TestFlight, bundle identifiers, pods and plugin registration included, and raised the Android app to target SDK 36. I built the in-app update flow and the version-gate endpoint that decides when an old client must upgrade.

Play ConsoleTestFlightSigningVersion gating
02

Performance is a field requirement, not a polish pass

SKALA is used by officers on site, on mid-range phones, often on a weak connection. Heavy filtering, sorting and CSV parsing run in isolates so the UI thread stays free. Photos are compressed and resized before upload. Lists are paginated and lazy-loaded behind shimmer placeholders, search is debounced, and draft and session caches mean an officer keeps their work when the signal drops.

IsolatesOffline-firstLazy loadingCaching
03

I have shipped to a government security bar

MyIBJKM holds citizens' family records and disaster-relief data, so it had to clear JKM's requirements. Local storage is SQLCipher with the key held in the Android Keystore. Sessions are signed tokens, the PHP endpoints use prepared statements, and the app ships with a locked-down network security config. On SKALA, authentication is JWT with refresh tokens alongside JKR's single sign-on.

SQLCipherKeystoreJWT refreshSSO
04

Architecture chosen so features land on both platforms at once

SKALA separates business logic from UI with BLoC across five user roles and more than forty endpoints, so a change ships to iOS and Android together rather than twice. On MyIBJKM the opposite call was right: two native codebases already served a live government app, so I upgraded them in place instead of risking a rewrite.

BLoCNative AndroidSwiftUIREST
